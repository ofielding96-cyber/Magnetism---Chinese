🧲
Permanent Magnet
永久磁铁
yǒng jiǔ cí tiě
▼
A material that produces its own magnetic field at all times. It does not need an external field to be magnetic. Examples: bar magnets, horseshoe magnets. Made from materials such as iron, steel, cobalt or nickel.
始终产生自身磁场的材料。不需要外部磁场就具有磁性。例子：条形磁铁、马蹄形磁铁。由铁、钢、钴或镍等材料制成。
★ Examples 例子: bar magnet 条形磁铁, horseshoe magnet 马蹄形磁铁
🔩
Induced Magnet
感应磁铁
gǎn yìng cí tiě
▼
A magnetic material (e.g. iron) that becomes a magnet only when placed in a magnetic field. Induced magnetism always causes attraction. When the external field is removed, the material quickly loses most or all of its magnetism.
只有当铁磁性材料（如铁）置于磁场中时才成为磁铁。感应磁性总是引起吸引力。当外部磁场移除时，材料会迅速失去大部分或全部磁性。
★ Induced magnetism ALWAYS attracts — never repels 感应磁性只有吸引，没有排斥
🔵🔴
Magnetic Poles
磁极
cí jí
▼
Every magnet has a north pole and a south pole — the places where the magnetic force is strongest. Like poles repel (N–N or S–S). Unlike poles attract (N–S). These are non-contact forces.
每块磁铁都有北极和南极——磁力最强的地方。同性磁极相互排斥（N-N或S-S）。异性磁极相互吸引（N-S）。这些是非接触力。
★ Like poles REPEL 同极排斥 · Unlike poles ATTRACT 异极吸引
🌐
Magnetic Field
磁场
cí chǎng
▼
The region around a magnet where a force acts on another magnet or magnetic material (iron, steel, cobalt, nickel). Represented by field lines. The field is strongest at the poles and weakens with distance. Field lines point from north to south outside the magnet.
磁铁周围对另一块磁铁或磁性材料（铁、钢、钴、镍）施加力的区域。用磁场线表示。磁场在磁极处最强，随距离增加而减弱。磁场线在磁铁外部从北极指向南极。
★ Field lines: N pole → S pole outside the magnet 磁场线从北极到南极
🧭
Magnetic Compass
磁罗盘
cí luó pán
▼
Contains a small bar magnet. The north pole of the needle is attracted towards geographic north because the Earth's core acts as a large magnet. The compass needle aligns with the Earth's magnetic field. Compasses can also be used to plot the field lines of a bar magnet.
内含一块小条形磁铁。指针的北极被吸引指向地理北方，因为地球核心像一块大磁铁。指南针与地球磁场对齐。指南针也可用于绘制条形磁铁的磁场线。
★ Earth's core must be magnetic — evidence from compass behaviour 地球核心必定具有磁性
⚡
Electromagnetism
电磁学
diàn cí xué
▼
When an electric current flows through a wire, a magnetic field is produced around that wire. The field strength depends on: the size of the current and the distance from the wire. The direction of the field depends on the direction of the current.
当电流流过导线时，导线周围会产生磁场。磁场强度取决于：电流大小和距离导线的距离。磁场方向取决于电流方向。
★ Bigger current = stronger field 电流越大，磁场越强 · Closer = stronger field 越近，越强
🌀
Solenoid
螺线管
luó xiàn guǎn
▼
A coil of wire carrying a current. Shaping a wire into a solenoid increases the strength of the magnetic field. The field inside is strong and uniform. The field around a solenoid has a similar shape to a bar magnet — with a north and south pole at each end.
通有电流的线圈。将导线绕成螺线管可以增强磁场强度。内部磁场强且均匀。螺线管周围的磁场与条形磁铁的形状相似——两端各有一个北极和南极。
★ More coils = stronger field 匝数越多，磁场越强
🔌
Electromagnet
电磁铁
diàn cí tiě
▼
A solenoid with an iron core. Adding the iron core greatly increases the strength of the magnetic field. Can be switched on and off by controlling the current. Used in cranes, electric bells, maglev trains and circuit breakers.
带铁芯的螺线管。加入铁芯可大大增强磁场强度。可通过控制电流来开关。用于起重机、电铃、磁悬浮列车和断路器。
★ Iron core: increases field strength greatly 铁芯大幅增强磁场 · Can be switched on/off 可开关
🌍
Earth's Magnetic Field
地球磁场
dì qiú cí chǎng
▼
The Earth has a magnetic field that extends into space. It behaves as if there is a large bar magnet inside the Earth. The compass needle points north because it is attracted to the Earth's magnetic south pole (which is near geographic north). This is evidence the Earth's core must be magnetic.
地球有一个延伸到太空的磁场，其行为就像地球内部有一块大条形磁铁。指针指向北方是因为它被地球的磁南极（位于地理北极附近）吸引。这证明地球核心必定具有磁性。
★ Geographic North = magnetic south pole of Earth 地理北极 = 地球磁南极
💪
The Motor Effect HT
电动机效应
diàn dòng jī xiào yìng
▼
When a conductor carrying a current is placed in a magnetic field, the magnet and conductor exert a force on each other. This is the motor effect. The force is increased by: increasing the current, increasing the magnetic field strength, or using a longer conductor.
当载流导体置于磁场中时，磁铁和导体之间会产生相互作用力。这就是电动机效应。通过增大电流、增强磁场强度或使用更长的导体可以增大力。
★ Force size depends on: current (I), field strength (B), length of conductor (l)
🤚
Fleming's Left-Hand Rule HT
弗莱明左手定则
fú lái míng zuǒ shǒu dìng zé
▼
A rule to find the direction of the force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field. Hold the left hand with: First finger = direction of magnetic Field (N→S), seCond finger = direction of Current, thuMb = direction of Motion (force). All three at right angles to each other.
确定载流导体在磁场中所受力方向的规则。用左手：食指指向磁场方向（N→S），中指指向电流方向，拇指指向运动（力）方向。三者互相垂直。
★ FBI: Field (finger) → Current (second finger) → Motion/force (thuMb)
⚙️
Electric Motor HT
电动机
diàn dòng jī
▼
A coil of wire carrying a current in a magnetic field tends to rotate. This is the basis of an electric motor. The two sides of the coil experience forces in opposite directions (using Fleming's left-hand rule), causing rotation. Motors convert electrical energy → kinetic energy.
在磁场中通有电流的线圈趋于旋转。这是电动机的基础。线圈的两侧受到方向相反的力（用弗莱明左手定则），导致旋转。电动机将电能转化为动能。
★ Electrical energy → kinetic energy 电能 → 动能
🔈
Loudspeakers HT
扬声器
yáng shēng qì
▼
Loudspeakers and headphones use the motor effect to convert variations in current in electrical circuits into pressure variations in sound waves. An alternating current through a coil in a magnetic field causes the coil (and attached cone) to vibrate back and forth, producing sound.
扬声器和耳机利用电动机效应将电路中电流的变化转换为声波的压力变化。通过磁场中线圈的交变电流使线圈（及连接的纸盆）来回振动，产生声音。
★ AC current → coil vibrates → cone vibrates → sound waves 交流电→线圈振动→纸盆振动→声波
⚡
Generator Effect HT
发电机效应
fā diàn jī xiào yìng
▼
If a conductor moves relative to a magnetic field, or if the magnetic field around a conductor changes, a potential difference is induced. If the conductor is in a complete circuit, a current flows. This is the generator effect (electromagnetic induction). The induced current opposes the change that caused it.
如果导体相对于磁场运动，或导体周围的磁场发生变化，则会产生感应电动势。如果导体处于完整电路中，则会产生电流。这就是发电机效应（电磁感应）。感应电流反对引起它的变化。
★ Increase induced pd/current: move faster, stronger field, more coil turns 提高感应电动势：加快速度、增强磁场、增加匝数
🔄
Alternator & Dynamo HT
交流发电机与直流发电机
jiāo liú fā diàn jī yǔ zhí liú fā diàn jī
▼
Both use the generator effect. An alternator uses slip rings to produce alternating current (AC) — the output reverses direction continuously. A dynamo uses a split-ring commutator to produce direct current (DC) — the output always flows in one direction.
两者都使用发电机效应。交流发电机使用滑环产生交流电（AC）——输出持续反向。直流发电机使用分裂环换向器产生直流电（DC）——输出始终单向流动。
★ Alternator → AC (slip rings) · Dynamo → DC (split-ring commutator)
🎤
Microphone HT
麦克风
mài kè fēng
▼
A microphone uses the generator effect to convert pressure variations in sound waves into variations in current in electrical circuits. Sound waves make a diaphragm vibrate, which moves a coil in a magnetic field, inducing a varying current. This is the reverse of a loudspeaker.
麦克风利用发电机效应将声波的压力变化转换为电路中的电流变化。声波使振膜振动，振膜带动磁场中的线圈运动，从而感应出变化的电流。这与扬声器的工作原理相反。
★ Microphone is the REVERSE of a loudspeaker 麦克风与扬声器工作原理相反
🔁
Transformer HT
变压器
biàn yā qì
▼
A device with a primary coil and secondary coil wound on an iron core. An alternating current in the primary coil creates a changing magnetic field in the iron core, which induces a voltage in the secondary coil. A step-up transformer increases voltage (more secondary turns). A step-down transformer decreases voltage (fewer secondary turns).
由初级线圈和次级线圈绕在铁芯上构成的装置。初级线圈中的交变电流在铁芯中产生变化的磁场，从而在次级线圈中感应出电压。升压变压器提高电压（次级匝数更多）。降压变压器降低电压（次级匝数更少）。
★ Step-up: Vs > Vp (more secondary turns) · Step-down: Vs < Vp (fewer secondary turns)

🌐 Magnetic Field Lines — 磁场线
AQA 4.7.1.2 — What you need to know about field lines
You must be able to draw and describe the magnetic field pattern of a bar magnet. Key points:

Direction — field lines go from the North pole to the South pole outside the magnet.
方向——磁场线在磁铁外部从北极到南极。
Strength — the closer together the lines are, the stronger the field. The field is strongest at the poles.
强度——磁场线越密集，磁场越强。磁场在磁极处最强。
Plotting — a compass can be used to plot field lines. The north end of the compass needle points in the direction of the field at that point.
绘制——可以用指南针绘制磁场线。指南针北端指向该点的磁场方向。
Never cross — magnetic field lines never cross each other.
不相交——磁场线永远不会相互交叉。
⚡ Electromagnetism — 电磁学
AQA 4.7.2.1 — Current, fields and solenoids
Three things to be able to describe clearly:
1. Current creates a field — a wire carrying a current produces a circular magnetic field around it. The greater the current, the stronger the field. The field is strongest close to the wire.
导线中的电流产生圆形磁场。电流越大，磁场越强。靠近导线处磁场最强。
2. Solenoid — wrapping wire into a coil (solenoid) makes the field stronger and more uniform inside. The field outside looks like a bar magnet.
将导线绕成线圈（螺线管）使内部磁场更强且更均匀。外部磁场看起来像条形磁铁。
3. Electromagnet — adding an iron core to a solenoid massively increases the field strength. It can be switched on and off. More coils of wire = stronger field. More current = stronger field.
在螺线管中加入铁芯可大幅增强磁场强度。可以开关。线圈匝数越多或电流越大 = 磁场越强。
🌍 Earth's Magnetic Field — 地球磁场
AQA 4.7.1.2 — Evidence from compass behaviour
The Earth has a magnetic field that surrounds the whole planet. A compass needle points north because the Earth's core acts as a large magnet. This behaviour of compasses is evidence that the core of the Earth must be magnetic.
地球有一个包围整个星球的磁场。指南针指北是因为地球核心像一块大磁铁。指南针的这种行为是地球核心必定具有磁性的证据。
EXAM TIP 考试提示
The geographic North Pole is actually near the Earth's magnetic south pole — that is why the north end of a compass needle points that way (opposite poles attract).
地理北极实际上靠近地球的磁南极——这就是指南针北端指向那个方向的原因（异极相吸）。
💪 The Motor Effect & F = BIl — 电动机效应与公式 F = BIl HT
AQA 4.7.2.2 — The only equation you must recall in this topic
When a conductor carrying a current is placed in a magnetic field, a force acts on the conductor. The size of this force is given by:
F = B × I × l
F = force (N) 力（牛顿）
B = magnetic flux density (T, tesla) 磁通量密度（特斯拉）
I = current (A) 电流（安培）
l = length of conductor in the field (m) 导体在磁场中的长度（米）
WORKED EXAMPLE 解题示例
A conductor of length 0.05 m carries a current of 3 A in a field of magnetic flux density 2 T. Calculate the force.
F = B × I × l = 2 × 3 × 0.05 = 0.3 N
一根长0.05 m的导体在磁通量密度为2 T的磁场中通有3 A的电流。计算力。
F = B × I × l = 2 × 3 × 0.05 = 0.3 N

⚡ The Generator Effect — 发电机效应 HT
AQA 4.7.3.1 — Inducing a potential difference
A potential difference (and current if in a circuit) is induced when:
A conductor moves relative to a magnetic field
导体相对于磁场运动
The magnetic field changes around a stationary conductor
静止导体周围的磁场发生变化
To increase the induced pd/current: move the conductor faster, use a stronger magnet, use more turns of wire. Reversing the direction of movement or the field reverses the direction of the induced current.
增大感应电动势/电流：加快导体速度、使用更强的磁铁、使用更多匝数。反转运动方向或磁场方向会反转感应电流方向。
🔁 Transformers & the National Grid — 变压器与国家电网 HT
AQA 4.7.3.4 — How transformers work and the transformer equation
A transformer uses electromagnetic induction. An AC current in the primary coil creates a changing magnetic field in the iron core, which induces a voltage in the secondary coil.
Vp / Vs = np / ns
Vp = primary voltage 初级电压 (V)  |  Vs = secondary voltage 次级电压 (V)
np = primary turns 初级匝数  |  ns = secondary turns 次级匝数
STEP-UP 升压
More secondary turns (ns > np)
Vs > Vp — voltage increases
次级匝数多，电压升高
STEP-DOWN 降压
Fewer secondary turns (ns < np)
Vs < Vp — voltage decreases
次级匝数少，电压降低
For a 100% efficient transformer: Vp × Ip = Vs × Is (power in = power out)
对于100%高效的变压器：输入功率 = 输出功率。国家电网在超高压下输电以减少电能损耗（P = I²R，电流越小损耗越少）。
★ National Grid uses step-up transformers at power stations and step-down transformers near homes 国家电网在发电站使用升压变压器，在住宅附近使用降压变压器
Equations
公式 — AQA Higher Tier: two equations for Magnetism · 两个磁学公式
📋 Higher Tier Equations: MUST RECALL = memorise and know how to apply. GIVEN = provided on the equation sheet.
必须记忆的公式 vs 考试时提供的公式。
Force on a Conductor (Motor Effect) 导体所受的力（电动机效应） MUST RECALL
AQA 4.7.2.2 — Apply when a current-carrying conductor is in a magnetic field
F = B × I × l
F = force 力 (N, newtons 牛顿)
B = magnetic flux density 磁通量密度 (T, tesla 特斯拉)
I = current 电流 (A, amperes 安培)
l = length of conductor in the field 导体在磁场中的长度 (m, metres 米)
WORKED EXAMPLE 解题示例
A wire of length 0.12 m carries a current of 5 A. The magnetic flux density is 0.3 T. Calculate the force.
F = B × I × l = 0.3 × 5 × 0.12 = 0.18 N
一根长0.12 m的导线通有5 A的电流，磁通量密度为0.3 T。计算力。
F = 0.3 × 5 × 0.12 = 0.18 N
Transformer Equation 变压器公式 GIVEN
AQA 4.7.3.4 — Relates the voltages and number of turns in each coil
Vp / Vs = np / ns
Vp = primary (input) voltage 初级（输入）电压 (V)
Vs = secondary (output) voltage 次级（输出）电压 (V)
np = number of turns on primary coil 初级线圈匝数
ns = number of turns on secondary coil 次级线圈匝数
WORKED EXAMPLE 解题示例
A transformer has 200 primary turns and 1000 secondary turns. The primary voltage is 50 V. Find the secondary voltage.
Vp/Vs = np/ns → 50/Vs = 200/1000 → Vs = 50 × 1000/200 = 250 V
变压器有200匝初级线圈和1000匝次级线圈，初级电压为50 V。求次级电压。
Vs = 50 × 1000/200 = 250 V（升压变压器）
Transformer Power Equation 变压器功率公式 GIVEN
AQA 4.7.3.4 — For a 100% efficient transformer, power in = power out
Vp × Ip = Vs × Is
Ip = primary current 初级电流 (A)
Is = secondary current 次级电流 (A)
When voltage is stepped up, current is stepped down (and vice versa) to conserve power.
